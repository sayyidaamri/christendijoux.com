    C—01 · components/webgl/dither-hero.js

    DitherHeroDitherHero

    The dithered organic sphere behind the hero. It breathes, follows the cursor like a loupe and dissolves as you scroll.

    Why
    I wanted an identity with no photography: two colours, one algorithm. A video would weigh megabytes; the shader weighs a few kilobytes.
    How
    Raw WebGL, one full-screen triangle. A raymarched sphere warped by 3D noise, lit from its normal, then quantised with an 8×8 Bayer matrix. Pixel size scales with the screen so phones do far less work.
    • WebGL
    • GLSL
    • Raymarching
    • Bayer 8×8
    Relevance★★★
    CostMedium. Paused off-screen, half resolution on phones.

    Verdict — The centrepiece — it gives the site its face. Next time: a low-power mode driven by the GPU, not the screen size.

    C—02 · components/ui/kinetic.js

    SplitText · proximity weightSplitText · proximity weight

    Headings reveal letter by letter, and their weight swells as the cursor comes near.

    Why
    Give type depth without images or video: the words themselves become the interactive layer.
    How
    Words are split into masked letters and slid in. A variable font's wght axis is driven by distance to the cursor, smoothed each frame. The loop sleeps when nothing moves.
    • Variable fonts
    • rAF
    • IntersectionObserver
    Relevance★★★
    CostLow. No loop while idle.

    Verdict — Great effect-to-cost ratio and accessible: the full text stays available to screen readers. Off on touch screens.

    C—03 · components/ui/scroll-words.js

    ScrollWordsScrollWords

    A paragraph that lights up word by word as you scroll.

    Why
    Slow the reading of key sentences: you read them at the speed they appear.
    How
    Each word's opacity is tied to the paragraph's scroll progress. Minimum opacity 0.38 to keep WCAG contrast.
    • Scroll progress
    • CSS
    Relevance★★★
    CostVery low.

    Verdict — Effective but very common on award sites. Use once or twice per page, no more.

    C—04 · components/ui/pixel-image.js

    PixelImagePixelImage

    Images resolve from big pixels to full definition. On hover the colour original opens in a circle from the cursor.

    Why
    Every image is dithered black and white to stay on brand; this still lets you see the real thing.
    How
    Canvas 2D: the image is drawn tiny then scaled up without smoothing, in 7 steps (64 → 1 px). The reveal is a CSS circle clip-path positioned with custom properties.
    • Canvas 2D
    • clip-path
    • IntersectionObserver
    Relevance★★★
    CostLow. 7 draws, then nothing.

    Verdict — Solves a real conflict (brand vs. readable projects). On touch screens the original reveals itself after the animation.

    ManifestoBandManifestoBand

    C—06 · components/cursor.js

    CustomCursorCustomCursor

    A dot and a reticle replace the cursor. Contextual labels and magnetic buttons.

    Why
    Make navigation feel tactile and hint at actions (“View”, “Open”) without more text in the UI.
    How
    Two elements eased toward the pointer at different speeds (the reticle lags). Any element opts in with data-cursor="Text" or data-magnetic.
    • rAF easing
    • Data attributes
    Relevance★★★
    CostVery low.

    Verdict — Pleasant attribute-based API. Disabled on touch and reduced motion: the system cursor comes back.

    C—08 · components/ui/expandable.js

    Expandable · pixel typeExpandable · pixel type

    The mobile “Read more”. On open, pixels fly from the folded edge and assemble the hidden words line by line, then the real text takes over.

    Why
    On phones, long entries were cut with no way to read on. They needed to expand, on theme: pixels, two colours.
    How
    Hidden lines are measured where the browser laid them out, redrawn on an off-screen canvas and sampled into 2 px dots. Each dot flies to its spot with a delay proportional to its height while a CSS mask uncovers the real text.
    • Canvas 2D
    • Range API
    • CSS mask
    Relevance★★★
    CostLow and short-lived (≈ 1.2 s), then nothing.

    Verdict — A useful detail AND a signature. Honours reduced motion. On desktop the full text is simply shown.

    live

    Two colours, no “…”. This paragraph is folded: click “Read more” and watch the pixels leave the edge, fall and settle one by one onto the shape of the letters, line after line, until they write the hidden words. Close it: the text breaks back into pixels that fly off upward. On a phone this is how every long entry opens.

    C—09 · components/ui/counter.js

    CounterCounter

    Numbers that roll up to their value (projects, repositories…).

    Why
    Give weight to key figures and rhythm to page headers.
    How

    • rAF
    • IntersectionObserver
    Relevance★★★
    CostVery low.

    Verdict — Useful but ordinary. It earns its place, nothing more.
