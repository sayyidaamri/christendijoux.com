    Fjordline InstituteFjordline Institute

    A headless platform for a coastal research institute

    Year2025
    StatusDelivered
    TypeClient work
    LanguagesTypeScript · Next.js · React · GraphQL · WordPress
    [ 01 / 05 ]·About the project

    Full redesign and merger of two WordPress sites into a headless Next.js platform. Structured migration of 10+ content types, a three-layer cache (~50 ms), advanced SEO and a handover plan for the client team.

    Fjordline Institute publishes research on coastal ecosystems. Its two ageing WordPress sites had drifted apart in design, content model and performance.

    I merged them into a single headless platform: WordPress stays the editing tool, Next.js renders everything, and a three-layer cache keeps pages under 50 ms worldwide.
