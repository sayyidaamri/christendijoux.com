    Internal software and AI for a ship chandler

    Year2026
    StatusIn progress
    TypeProfessional work
    LanguagesPython · TypeScript · AL
    [ 01 / 05 ]·About the project

    Systems engineer in a four-person IT team: I design and build the internal software, applied AI and dockside tools of a Norwegian ship chandler with three depots.

    Since January 2026 I have been the systems & AI engineer at Halvorsen Marine, a ship chandler supplying vessels from three depots along the west coast, inside a four-person IT team. My job: find where software and AI save time, then ship it. I designed and built, end to end, the teams' internal platform, a dockside order-tracking tool and a tablet app for crews that plugs straight into the ERP.

    I am also building an AI copilot that answers plain-language questions from ERP data, so a buyer can ask “what did the Nordkapp order last spring?” instead of opening four screens.
