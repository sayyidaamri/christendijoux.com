    01Who is responsible

    The controller for personal data processed on this site is Kaspian Rowe, Bergen, Norway. Contact: hello@kaspianrowe.dev.

    02What is collected

    • Contact form: name, email and message, only when you send it.
    • Analytics (only if you accept): anonymised page views, device type, country.
    • Error reports: technical data about crashes, with no personal content.

    03Cookies & local storage

    Two kinds of storage are used. Necessary ones remember your language, your cookie choice and your star-hunt progress (localStorage, never sent anywhere). Optional analytics cookies are set only after you click “Accept”. You can change your mind at any time via “Manage cookies” in the footer.

    04Retention

    Messages are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted. Analytics data is aggregated and kept for 14 months.

    05Your rights

    Under the GDPR you may access, correct, delete or export your data, and object to its processing. Write to hello@kaspianrowe.dev; you may also complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

    06The assistant

    The chat assistant runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server or a third-party AI provider.

    07Changes

    This policy may evolve with the site. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
